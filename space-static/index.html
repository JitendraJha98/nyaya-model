Nyaya

The statute retriever behind an open Indian legal guidance system. Ask in English, Hindi or Hinglish and see the sections of current Indian law (BNS / BNSS / BSA in force since 1 July 2024, the Constitution and twenty-four other acts) your question resolves to. Old IPC / CrPC numbers are bridged through the official mapping tables. Runs entirely in your browser.

⚖️ Not legal advice. Nyaya provides legal information. The practice of law in India is reserved to advocates enrolled under the Advocates Act, 1961. Consult a licensed advocate for anything consequential. Free legal aid: NALSA / DLSA (helpline 15100).
